Who We Are

Anubhav NGO

Educational & Social Welfare Society

Anubhav is committed to empowering communities through skill development, sustainable livelihood creation, and preservation of India's cultural heritage. We work with women, youth, and marginalized communities to create opportunities that promote self-reliance and dignity.

Mithila Paintings

Traditional Art, Contemporary Products

Mithila art, also known as Madhubani art, is a centuries-old tradition from Bihar. Through Project Hunar, we transform these ancient designs into modern products while preserving the cultural legacy and empowering artisans.
