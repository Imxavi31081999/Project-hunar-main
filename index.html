01 — About Project Hunar

Some of the most beautiful things in the world are made by hands that were once told they had nothing to offer.

Project Hunar was born from a simple belief that every woman, regardless of where she comes from, carries within her the capacity to create something extraordinary. What she needs is the space, the training, and someone to believe in her before she believes in herself.

Since its inception under Anubhav Educational and Social Welfare Society, Project Hunar has trained women from underserved communities across Delhi in tailoring, stitching, garment making, crochet, and the ancient art of Madhubani painting. These are not just skills. They are livelihoods. And behind every product in this catalogue is a woman who built hers from scratch.

Every stitch is intentional. Every piece is made with care. And every order placed with Project Hunar goes directly toward sustaining the livelihood of a woman who earned it.

02 — The Hands Behind The Craft

Every workshop needs a soul. Ours has one.

Project Hunar's production is guided by our in-house masterji, a seasoned craft professional whose expertise spans decades of tailoring, garment construction, and quality finishing.

Under his guidance, what begins as a woman's first uncertain stitch becomes a confident, finished product that meets professional standards. He is the reason our quality is consistent. And he is the reason our women grow, not just as artisans, but as professionals.

When you order from Project Hunar, you are not just getting a well-made product. You are getting the result of mentorship, patience, and craft passed down with intention.

04 — Madhubani Collection

There is an art form that has survived centuries in the villages of Bihar's Mithila region.

Passed down from mother to daughter, painted on walls, floors, and fabric, Madhubani art carries within it the stories, rituals, and beliefs of generations of women who had no other canvas. Our collection is developed under the guidance of Anuradha Jha, a disciple of Padma Shri awardee Sita Devi, one of the most celebrated exponents of Mithila art.

Every piece is hand-painted — the fish, the lotus, the sun, the peacock, the bride and groom — each motif carrying a story thousands of years old, now living on a saree, a shirt, a lamp, a diary. All pieces are available for custom orders. Bulk orders welcome from 25 pieces onwards.
